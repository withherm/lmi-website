## 1. What changed, so you know what to attack

The container outline used to be a CSS border pushed around by an
`feDisplacementMap`. That tore: a displacement filter moves a rasterised line by
a quantised amount, so a 3px border came out in steps with visible breaks.

It is now an SVG path used as a `border-image`, generated by
`prototypes/tools/gen_edges.py` into `src/styles/edges.css`, applied to eleven
container selectors with `border-image-slice: 24`, `border-image-width: 24px`
and `border-image-repeat: stretch`.

Three other things went in around it: a washed band behind grouped objects
(`.g-wash`), a multiply pooling inside pale fills (`#pool`), and a screen wash
inside solid navy fills (`#wash`).

## 3. The checks

**3.1 The short-container failure.** `border-image-slice: 24` at
`border-image-width: 24px` reserves 24px at each end of every edge. A container
shorter or narrower than roughly 55px has no room for a straight run and the
corner slices collide, which renders as a pinched or doubled corner.

Enumerate every element matching the selector list in `edges.css` across every
route, at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440. Report any whose rendered height or
width is under 60px, with route, viewport, selector, and measured box. This is
the check I most expect to surface something.

**3.2 Generated-file drift.** Run `python3 prototypes/tools/gen_edges.py` and
diff against the committed `src/styles/edges.css`. They must match. If they do
not, the committed file has been hand-edited and the generator is a lie.

**3.3 Filter regions.** `#pool`, `#wash` and `#paper` were given explicit
regions because `feTurbulence` is a generator and the default `-10%/120%` paints
noise outside the element. That shipped once as a grey halo around every washed
surface. Verify every filter in `src/layouts/Layout.astro` either clamps its
region or has a reason not to, and screenshot at 3x or better to confirm no
spill remains around washed elements.

**3.4 `overflow:hidden` on `.g-wash`.** The band needs it so the displaced edge
does not cause horizontal overflow. Confirm it clips nothing that matters, and
in particular that no `position:sticky` element sits inside a `.g-wash` section
on any route. `.enrol` is sticky and I believe it is in a different section, but
I have not proven it.

**3.5 `background-clip: padding-box`.** Every drawn container now has it.
Confirm nothing depended on the background painting under the border area, and
that no container shows a hairline gap between its background and its drawn
line at any zoom level from 100% to 400%.

**3.6 Focus and hover.** Hover and the pressed states swap `--drawn-src` rather
than a border colour. Tab through every interactive drawn container, on every
route, and confirm the focus indicator is still visible and not obscured. WCAG
2.4.7 and 2.4.11.

**3.7 Reflow and contrast, again.** 320 CSS px on every route, no horizontal
overflow, WCAG 1.4.10. Non-text contrast on every control boundary, 3:1, WCAG
1.4.11. I measured chips, tabs and the credential input at 3.70 on white and
3.36 on off-white. Verify, and check anything I missed.

**3.8 Cost.** Report the CSS payload delta, gzipped, and whether the removal of
the border filters changed scroll or paint cost measurably. I measured 339ms
against 365ms over 20 frames on the home page. Confirm or correct.

**3.9 Anything else that is actually broken.** Console errors, failed requests,
build warnings, dead CSS, selectors matching nothing, tokens defined and never
used.
